! Title: gtm-rr - Google Tag Manager
! Description: Notes for googletagmanager_gtm.js, which replaces uBlock
!              Origin's resource of the same name.
! Expires: 7 days
! Homepage: https://github.com/ryanbr/gtm-rr
!
! On the sites uBO's own lists already cover, this list is not needed at all.
! Installing the resource is the whole of it.

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! uBO ships a resource called googletagmanager_gtm.js, and its default lists
! send both of Google's loaders to that name:
!
!   privacy.txt  ||googletagmanager.com/gtag/js$script,xhr,
!                  redirect=googletagmanager_gtm.js:5
!   privacy.txt  ||googletagmanager.com/gtm.js$script,
!                  redirect=googletagmanager_gtm.js:5,domain=~nerc.com
!
! A user resource replaces a built-in of the same name - uBO awaits its own
! loadBuiltinResources first and parses the userResourcesLocation text after it
! into the same map - so installing ours under that name is all that is needed
! for those rules to serve it. No filter below is required for that.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Server-side tagging puts the same loaders on the site's own domain, where
! uBO's rules do not reach, and the path is what identifies them. One rule per
! site, since the subdomain is the site's choice:
!
! ||sgtm.example.com/gtm.js$script,redirect=googletagmanager_gtm.js
! ||metrics.example.com/gtag/js$script,redirect=googletagmanager_gtm.js
!
! The resource reads the container id and the data layer's name out of the
! query either way, and which loader it is standing in for out of the path.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Where a redirect cannot land at all - an inlined bundle, or a path a rule
! cannot match - the resource has nothing to stand in front of, and the
! scriptlet form would be answering for a loader that is still running. Use
! ga-optout instead, which turns on Google's own opt-out and leaves the bundle
! to disable itself. See filters/ga.txt.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Deliberately left alone:
!
!   googletagmanager.com/ns.html        the <noscript> iframe, which nothing
!                                       in the page reads, so blocking it is
!                                       the whole treatment
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
