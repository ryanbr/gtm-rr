! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! NO FILTER IS NEEDED FOR THIS ONE.
!
! uBO's own lists already redirect Google Publisher Tag:
!
!   ||securepubads.g.doubleclick.net/tag/js/gpt.js$script,redirect=googletagservices_gpt.js:5
!   ||pagead2.googlesyndication.com/tag/js/gpt.js$script,redirect=googletagservices_gpt.js:5
!
! and a user resource of a built-in's name replaces the built-in - uBO awaits
! loadBuiltinResources first and parses userResourcesLocation after it, into
! the same Map. So installing this repo is the whole of it: those rules start
! serving this instead, on every site they already cover.
!
! Which also means it has to be at least as safe as what it displaces. The
! reason it exists is that theirs throws: 28 documented, publisher-facing GPT
! calls raise a TypeError on it rather than answering, and a stub that throws
! is worse than either a noop or the real file, because allowing and blocking
! both leave a page coherent.
!
! The surface here was enumerated from the real gpt.js in a browser, not
! copied from another resource: googletag 29 names, pubads() 51,
! defineSlot(...) 37, companionAds() 14, content() 6, and
! secureSignalProviders 4 - which is NOT an array, whatever the name suggests.
!
! It answers everything and does nothing. Two rules it keeps from the rest of
! this repo:
!
!   no invented ids       getCorrelator() answers '' and
!                         getTagSessionCorrelator() answers 0. The real ones
!                         join a page's requests together, and minting one
!                         here would be this resource creating the thing the
!                         blocking exists to prevent.
!   no signal collection  secureSignalProviders.push does nothing, so a third
!                         party's collectorFunction is never invoked and no
!                         encrypted signal is generated, let alone sent.
!                         Anything queued before this arrived is left alone,
!                         because draining it would run those collectors.
!
! It does answer the slot events a page branches on - slotRequested,
! slotResponseReceived, slotRenderEnded, slotOnload, impressionViewable - with
! isEmpty true, because a page that waits to find out whether its slot filled
! should be told, and a resource that registers the listener and never calls
! it leaves the page mid-decision. It does not build a google_ads_iframe_* in
! the slot: an event is a page's own question answered, an iframe carrying
! data-load-complete is a prop for something checking whether an ad rendered.
!
! To see it work: localStorage.setItem('gtm-rr-debug', 'verbose') and reload.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
