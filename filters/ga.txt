! Title: gtm-rr - Google Analytics opt-out
! Description: Turns on Google's own opt-out switches, for the bundles a
!              redirect cannot reach.
! Expires: 7 days
! Homepage: https://github.com/ryanbr/gtm-rr
!
! These filters do nothing on their own: the resource they name has to be
! installed through uBlock Origin's userResourcesLocation setting first. See the
! README.

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! This one replaces nothing, so it is a scriptlet and not a redirect: it has to
! run alongside a real Google bundle, before that bundle sends anything.
!
! Use it where blocking is not an option - the loader is served from the site's
! own domain, or from a proxied path, or the bundle is inlined in the page - so
! there is no third-party URL to match. The page keeps a real, working Google
! bundle that refuses to send, by its own check.
!
! example.com##+js(ga-optout)
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Applying it everywhere is a decision, not a default, so this list does not
! make it. It costs one function, one attribute and a flag per id on every
! page, and it is only of use where a Google bundle actually runs - which, with
! the usual lists active, is the pages that got through them.
!
! To take that decision:
!
! *##+js(ga-optout)
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Where the loader IS a matchable third-party URL, a redirect is the better
! treatment: nothing is fetched at all, rather than fetched and told to be
! quiet. See filters/gtm.txt for the container, and uBO's own
! google-analytics_analytics.js for the classic analytics.js.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
